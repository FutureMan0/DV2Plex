# Code of Conduct

DV2Plex is a small project run by volunteers. We want it to be a friendly place for
everyone who uses it or helps with it, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here digitize their family's tapes for the first time.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and may say no.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, threats, and demeaning comments about anyone's person or group.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information, including anything visible in their
  recordings, logs or screenshots, without permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after a maintainer asked you to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone
representing the project elsewhere. Report problems privately to the maintainer through
GitHub (the contact on the [maintainer's profile](https://github.com/FutureMan0), or a private
security report if it concerns safety). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove content, warn, or block people who break these rules.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
