              Welcome to DV2Plex
              
            
              
            
        
              
        
              

        
              
            
              Four short steps: language, where your films go, the camera, done. You can change everything later in the settings.
              
            
              
                
                
            
              
        
              

        
              
            
              
                
                
              
                    
                    
                
              
                
              The folder your Plex server reads films from. Plex needs to see it as a "Movies" library.
              
            
              
            
              
                
                
              
                    
                    
                
              
                
              Captures, merged and upscaled films. DV needs about 13 GB per hour of tape.
              
            
              
            
              
        
              

        
              
            
              Connect the camera over FireWire and switch it to playback (VCR).
              
            
              
            
        
              

        
              
            
              Ready. Put a tape in, enter its title and year on the Capture page and start the recording. The film reaches Plex through the Upscaling and Plex export pages, or by itself if you switch that on in the settings.
